# Spoken-safety certification corpus authority

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Tracking:** [#908](https://github.com/loomarr/loomarr/issues/908)
**Decision:** use **both** a deterministic public-corpus importer for clean controls and a consented known-script recording packager for positives and transformation slices.

This is an acquisition/authority recommendation, not a safety certificate, admission decision, or permission to collect, download, send, or play corpus media. It does not name or reproduce restricted-language policy content. “Commercial/private/model evaluation permitted” below means the cited copyright licence appears to allow the necessary reproduction, adaptation, and sharing; it is not a conclusion about privacy, personality, provider, employment, contract, or other rights.

## What certification must count

**Verified contract facts.** #908 requires a source-family-disjoint, immutable real-recording challenge; zero misses across at least 59 independent real positive source families; complete-source coverage; alias-blind review; exact audibility/timing truth; and observed false positives for every locked clean slice. The current design already treats a master, its alternate encodes, segments, and derivatives as one source family, and requires immutable source/media provenance and interval bounds ([issue #908](https://github.com/loomarr/loomarr/issues/908), [design §8.1](../../design.md#evidence-based-admission-certification-v61--supersedes-the-scalar-gate)).

**Statistical inference and proposed lock.** A family—not a clip, replay, codec, or edited derivative—is the unit of both the 59-positive recall denominator and clean false-positive (FP) denominator. With 59 zero-miss independent positive families, the exact one-sided 95% binomial recall lower bound is about 95.0%. This is why variants cannot inflate the count.

For the requested **1% observed-FP** clean gate, lock at least **100 independent clean source families**: at most one FP then has an observed rate of 1%. Call that only an observed rate. If the intended claim is instead a one-sided 95% confidence upper bound of at most 1% with zero observed FPs, lock at least **299** independent clean families. A nonzero-FP confidence claim needs a separately predeclared exact calculation and denominator. Each declared clean slice reports its own numerator/denominator; none borrows observations from another slice.

Positive transformations must be truth-bearing, not generic augmentation: accent/locale, noise, music, codec, clipping, and placement each need locked real-speaker positive examples with a reviewed audible interval. A source's dry master and all variants stay in one split and count once. The global 59-family gate does **not** prove recall for every cell; a cell needs its own denominator and, if hard-gated, its own threshold/confidence rule.

## Why no public corpus supplies the positive authority

**Verified absence of a sufficient claim.** The cited corpus material describes read/transcribed speech, licences, and sometimes alignment. It does not provide Loomarr-policy-labelled positives with reviewed prohibited audible intervals, nor prove music, clipping, codec, or placement coverage. A known transcript establishes intended words, not that they were audible in selected bytes. Public-domain status, an open licence, alignment, and model agreement do not establish safety truth.

**Inference.** A consented known-script pack is the only identified route that can make positive truth, rights scope, and transformations auditable without treating an open corpus as unreviewed restricted-language evidence. It must use real speakers—not TTS—to satisfy #908; synthetic controls remain development-only.

## Minimum deterministic authority artifacts

### Public-corpus importer (VCTK first)

The importer accepts only a reviewed **source-release authority**; it does not discover or download arbitrary media. Minimum inputs per case:

1. Source authority, release/version, licence URL/text hash, item URL, expected checksum/size when supplied, representation codec, and attribution/restriction record.
2. `sourceFamilyID` (VCTK speaker ID), stable item ID, immutable transcript hash, source-supplied locale/accent when present, and a one-split assignment.
3. Decoded source hash/duration and a bounded `[start,end)` interval; for VCTK the initial interval is the whole measured utterance, never an invented word timestamp.
4. Two blind attestations that transcript is fully audible in the interval and that it is clean under the locked private policy, with third-party adjudication on disagreement.
5. A rights-review decision for exact use, hosted processing if applicable, redistribution, attribution, retention/deletion, and reviewer/time.

Reject absent/duplicate speaker keys, transcript mismatch, non-positive/out-of-bounds intervals, missing hashes, unreviewed restrictions, masters in more than one split, and an alternate representation promoted to a new family. Emit only opaque aliases to reviewers/providers and keep audio out of Git.

### Consented known-script recording packager (positive authority)

The packager receives **already acquired** recordings; it creates no media and never turns a directory into consent. Minimum owner-authored inputs:

1. One signed/recorded consent and rights grant per real speaker covering collection, internal storage, deterministic transformations, private and hosted model evaluation, independent blind review, publication/redistribution choice, retention/deletion, withdrawal, and no endorsement. A private participant ID maps to opaque `sourceFamilyID`.
2. Versioned script ID/text hash and private policy mapping, recording session/take ID, consented locale/accent fields, and raw-media digest/format/duration.
3. A source-relative intended positive span plus two independent reviewer attestations of final audible `[start,end)`. Reviewers may correct timing or reject audibility; script alone does not create truth.
4. Deterministic recipe/tool/version/hash for every required slice: accent, locale, noise, music, codec, clipping, placement. Record mix source/rights, gain/clipping operation, codec/container parameters, padding/placement, input/output hashes, and translated output interval.
5. Family/split lock: every dry take, retake, cut, re-encode, mix, clipping, and placement derivative of one master shares family/split and supplies one statistical observation.

Hold a row if any grant, master digest, transformation provenance, complete coverage, interval, or reviewer/adjudicator attestation is missing. Apply the same rule to any music/noise asset whose licence does not cover the exact transformation and hosted processing.

## Recommendation and blockers

Implement **both** artifacts, beginning with VCTK clean importer and consented positive packager. VCTK's 110 speakers give a practical path to the 100-family observed-FP gate; the recording pack supplies the controlled 59 positive families and positive slices. Add LibriSpeech only after the importer proves the contract; keep VoxPopuli/Spoken Wikipedia as separately rights-reviewed expansion paths.

Block certification—and leave #908 shadow-only—until:

- a locked private-policy reviewer protocol and rights worksheet exist for every exact representation and hosted processor;
- at least 59 genuinely independent real positive families have reviewed exact intervals;
- at least 100 independently screened clean families support the observed 1% gate (or 299 zero-FP families for a one-sided 95% <=1% claim);
- a predeclared slice matrix has real positive coverage for every required transformation and non-overlapping family splits; and
- immutable media/provenance, blind labels, adjudications, and source/variant cluster checks exist, with no raw audio/private identities in Git, fixtures, public logs, or provider prompts.
